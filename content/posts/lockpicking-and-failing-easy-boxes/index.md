Happy very late Wednesday!

Since I haven't wrote about a box in a while, I decided to write about what I've been doing!

Lockpicking!?

It's been fun to say the least! So far I've (legally) been working on the Master No. 3 and 140! (Both are my locks again by the way!!). Struggled really hard in the beginning, as I didn't even understand the mechanism of the locks.

So there is pins? And they have different modes? I call them modes. Sometimes they stick and sometimes they are loose. 

Do you like my knowledge? It's bad. But, guess what.. I can unlock both locks at will!

Now I'm onto what's considered an intermediate lock! The American 1100. I've noticed that I am able to get a few pins! But this lock has a bunch of different mechanisms!

The Master No. 3 and 140 both have 4 pins, the other has 6. I looked this up, and apparently the 1100 even has false clicks and false sets. So there has been two times where the lock on the 1100 feels like it's going to budge, but it doesn't. If I understand it correctly, this is a security feature! It's really cool actually. 

I got a long way to go..
