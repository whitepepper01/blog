Balancing heavy lifting with the OSCP grind is its own kind of enumeration — find what works, log everything, iterate.

## The approach

- **Consistency beats intensity.** One box and one lifting session a day beats a burnout weekend.
- **Track the inputs.** I log calories and macros daily. High protein (~180–190g on a good day) keeps the lifts moving while I'm sitting at a terminal for hours.
- **Protect sleep.** Late-night root shells are fun, but recovery is where the gains (and the retention) actually happen.
