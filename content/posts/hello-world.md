Welcome. This is where I document the journey.

## What this is

I'm grinding toward the **OSCP** and building a career in offensive security. Instead of letting my writeups rot in an Obsidian vault, I'm publishing them here so I can:

- Keep myself accountable and track progress over time.
- Share the thinking behind each box — including the dead ends, not just the clean path.
- Build a public portfolio for the job hunt.

## What you'll find

- **Security** — machine walkthroughs, CTF and OSCP-style writeups, tooling notes.
- **Fitness & training** — lifting and nutrition logs.
- **Music & life** — whatever I'm into.

## How it's built

Static site with [Hugo](https://gohugo.io/) and the PaperMod theme, hosted free on GitHub Pages. I write posts in Markdown in Obsidian, drop them into `content/posts/`, commit, and push — the site rebuilds and deploys automatically.
